- **증거 데이터**:
  ```sql
  SELECT tr.stock_code, tr.profit_rate,
         ptp.days_after_sell, ptp.change_from_sell
  FROM trade_reviews tr
  JOIN post_trade_prices ptp ON ptp.review_id = tr.id
  WHERE tr.sell_reason='손절' AND ptp.days_after_sell IN (1,2,5)
  ORDER BY tr.sell_date, ptp.days_after_sell;
  ```
